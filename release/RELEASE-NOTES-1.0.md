# Offline DAoC True 1.65 v1.0

Download **Download-OfflineDAoC-True165-v1.0.zip**, extract it, and run
**GET-OFFLINE-DAOC-TRUE165.cmd**. Choose **Update my game** for an existing install or
**New install** for a fresh world. The launcher applies the new world content at the first **START SERVER**.

## What changed

- Tab cycles nearby enemies from closest to farthest. Left-clicks can reach enemies seen through gaps in large foreground models. Overhead names are still clickable.
- Companion buffs prioritize each party member's role. Healers can share baseline buff coverage when one runs short on concentration. AI Options can put the front line or casters and healers first.
- Runemasters maintain and recover their group bladeturn, and can combine their damage add with a Skald's chant. Skalds retry the selected combat chant after a blocked switch.
- Casters have two explicit choices below half power. They can finish PvE targets under 30% health or save all damage spells. Automatic behaves as before.
- Thane's Blood and seven other quests now support their equipment checks. Existing characters on the Ancient Axe step can equip their axe and continue.
- Escape clears your target while hovering a merchant item and keeps its tooltip available.
- Both tools are included under **tools**. The OfflineDAoC Database opens from **index.html** in a current browser and works offline. The Crafting Guide bundles a 64-bit Windows executable and Python/Tk source for other operating systems.

## Upgrading

The in-place update preserves your world database and saves. If local edits prevent the update, the downloader can create a separate v1.0 installation and transfer your progress. It keeps your old installation in place. Compatibility hashes cover v0.1 through v0.9, including v0.4.1.

See the [v1.0 changelog](https://github.com/zomboden/OfflineDAoC-True165/blob/v1.0/CHANGELOG.md#10) for the full list.
